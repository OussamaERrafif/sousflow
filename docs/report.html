Section 01

The Problem

The Souss-Massa region is Morocco's agricultural heartland, producing over 65% of the nation's citrus and olive exports. However, the region faces a critical water crisis. Groundwater levels have dropped dramatically over the past two decades, and traditional irrigation practices continue to waste significant volumes of water through over-irrigation, leaks, and lack of real-time monitoring.

Olive farmers in particular face a compounding challenge: irrigation system failures, such as pipe leaks, clogged drippers, or pump malfunctions, often go undetected for days. By the time the damage is visible on the trees, yield loss is already irreversible. There is currently no affordable, localized solution that combines real-time monitoring with intelligent anomaly detection tailored for the region's farms.

Core challenge: Farmers lack the tools to detect irrigation anomalies early, leading to water waste, crop stress, and financial losses that threaten the sustainability of olive farming in the region.

Section 02

The Solution

SoussFlow is a smart irrigation platform that uses IoT sensors and machine learning to detect anomalies in real time and provide intelligent irrigation recommendations. The platform is designed specifically for olive farms in the Souss-Massa region.

Anomaly Detection Engine

At the core of SoussFlow lies a real-time anomaly detection system that continuously analyzes sensor data — soil moisture, flow rates, pressure levels, and environmental conditions. Using machine learning models trained on regional agricultural patterns, the system identifies deviations that indicate potential problems:

Leak Detection

Identifies abnormal pressure drops and flow rate inconsistencies that signal pipe leaks or dripper failures across irrigation zones.

Pump Malfunction Alerts

Monitors pump performance metrics and detects degradation patterns before complete failure occurs.

Clogging Prediction

Analyzes flow patterns to predict dripper clogging events, enabling preventive maintenance scheduling.

Soil Moisture Anomalies

Detects unexpected soil moisture levels that may indicate broken lines, waterlogging, or sensor faults.

Smart Irrigation Scheduling

Beyond anomaly detection, SoussFlow provides zone-level irrigation recommendations based on real-time soil data, weather forecasts, and crop water requirements. This ensures each olive tree zone receives precisely the water it needs — nothing more, nothing less.

Section 03

Field Research & Validation

To ensure SoussFlow addresses real-world needs, we conducted on-the-ground research in the Souss-Massa region, visiting both supply-side stakeholders and end-user farmers.

Visit to Irrigation Material Wholesaler

We visited a major irrigation equipment wholesaler in the Agadir region to understand the current market landscape for drip irrigation systems, sensors, and automation components.

  • Confirmed that most farmers purchase basic drip systems without any monitoring or automation capabilities.
  • Identified compatible sensor hardware (soil moisture probes, flow meters, pressure sensors) that can integrate with our IoT architecture at accessible price points.
  • Learned that demand for "smart" add-ons is growing, but no local supplier currently offers a complete software platform to complement the hardware.
  • Established potential partnership opportunities for bundled hardware + software packages.
Supply Gap Hardware Compatibility Partnership Potential
Farmer Interview — Irrigation Workflow

We spoke with an olive farmer who walked us through his complete irrigation workflow, from scheduling to maintenance routines.

  • Irrigation scheduling is entirely manual, based on personal experience and visual inspection of the soil and trees — no data-driven decisions.
  • Leak detection happens only when water bills spike or visible damage appears on trees — often weeks after the issue begins.
  • The farmer expressed strong interest in receiving WhatsApp alerts for anomalies, as he is already comfortable using the app daily.
  • Maintenance is reactive rather than preventive: drippers are cleaned only after visible clogging causes uneven watering.
Manual Processes Late Detection WhatsApp-Ready

Key takeaway: Both supply-side and demand-side research confirmed a clear market gap. The technology and hardware exist, but there is no affordable, integrated platform that brings anomaly detection and smart irrigation together for the local market.
